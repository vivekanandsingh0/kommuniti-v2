What is a Kore?

A Kore is your
neighbourhood
as it should be.

Not a WhatsApp group. Not a resident association. Not a NGO. A Kore is a community of 5–20 people who gather weekly, act together, earn together, and govern their 1km² zone of the world.

🗓Weekly Gatherings — The Heartbeat
Every Kore meets once a week at its L7 gathering point. Physical, online, or hybrid. Missing gathering = losing KSS points. Consistent gathering = the foundation of everything that follows — KO Coins, KOKO Store, campaigns.
🏛6 Rotating Roles — Democratic Governance
Koordinator · Kreator · Keeper · Kare Lead · Kronicler · Konnector. Every 6 months, roles rotate. No permanent leaders. Everyone leads. Everyone serves. This is what makes a Kore different from every other group chat or resident association you've been in.
📍Issue Tagging — Your Zone, Your Responsibility
Members photograph civic problems — waste, water, roads, power. Every tag is pinned to a DigiPin L9 cell on the map. 3 co-tags = Heating. 10+ = Campaign. Campaign solved = permanent green pixel. Yours forever.
💰Real Earnings — Not Points, Not Badges
KO Coins earned through gathering, tagging, and deliveries are real money — withdrawable via UPI to your bank account. Your Kore's KSS score determines your minting rate. Forest Kores earn up to 500 KO Coins per member per day.
🛒KOKO Store — Your Kore's Marketplace
Every Kore member can list products and services on the KOKO Store — artisan goods, organic produce, skills, experiences. Sellers earn 75–80% of every sale. Buyers use KO Coins for up to 40% off. Your neighbourhood becomes an economy.
