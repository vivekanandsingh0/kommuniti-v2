\subsection{1. Executive summary (30-second pitch)}

\textbf{KO Reads is collaborative publishing for the internet age.}


Authors share \textbf{unreleased book drafts} on Kommuniti. Readers don’t just consume --- they \textbf{highlight text in the margins}, complete \textbf{bounty tasks} (research, taglines, dialogue, plot ideas), vote in \textbf{community polls}, post \textbf{fan theories}, and join \textbf{story circles}. When a contribution is genuinely useful, the author \textbf{accepts it}, credits the reader on the book, and rewards them with \textbf{KO Coins}.


\textbf{One line:} \textit{Read unreleased ideas. Shape the final book.}


\textbf{Secondary line:} \textit{Public drafts · Living margins}


\medskip\hrule\medskip

\subsection{2. What problem does KO Reads solve?}

\begin{longtable}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
For readers & For authors & For Kommuniti \\ \hline
Access to ideas before they’re finished & Real feedback before publication & Democratised learning \& shared knowledge \\ \hline
Recognition and KO Coins for sharp thinking & A community that sharpens the work & A decentralised library built over time \\ \hline
Belonging to a book’s creation story & Retained voice and final say & Engagement beyond passive scrolling \\
\hline
\end{longtable}


KO Reads is \textbf{not} a homework platform, a fan-fiction site, or a voting mob that overrides the author. It is a \textbf{slow, thoughtful, author-led} space where curiosity is rewarded.


\medskip\hrule\medskip

\subsection{3. Core messaging pillars (use these everywhere)}

\subsubsection{Pillar 1 --- Curiosity first}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Designed for \textbf{discovery}, not homework.
  \item Readers are invited to \textbf{notice what the author missed} --- a question, a source, a lived example, a contradiction.
  \item Tone: literary, calm, intelligent --- not hype or gamification noise.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Pillar 2 --- Author-led}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Authors \textbf{keep ownership} of voice and decisions.
  \item Readers contribute; authors \textbf{accept, reject, or mark as valuable}.
  \item The author is the editor-in-chief; the community is the sharpening stone.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Pillar 3 --- Rewarded value}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Useful work earns \textbf{recognition} (public credits on the book) and \textbf{KO Coins}.
  \item Quality over volume: \textit{“Leave the kind of note an author remembers.”}
  \item Best contributions are \textbf{precise}: missing examples, sharper questions, sources, contradictions, lived detail.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Pillar 4 --- Unreleased / draft culture}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Books are shown as \textbf{Drafts} --- manuscripts in progress, not finished products.
  \item Readers get \textbf{early access} to ideas the world hasn’t seen yet.
  \item Visual metaphor: \textbf{the margin} --- the space beside the official story where quieter truth lives.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{5. How KO Reads works --- user journey (for video scripts)}

\subsubsection{Journey A --- The curious reader (60--90 sec explainer)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Land on \texttt{/koreads}} --- dark literary UI, purple accents, featured manuscript card.
  \item \textbf{Pick a book} --- e.g. \textit{Margins of the Common} --- cover shows “Draft”, genre, “Open” badge if accepting contributions.
  \item \textbf{Read a chapter} --- cream manuscript page, serif body text (Cormorant Garamond feel).
  \item \textbf{Highlight a passage} --- select text, write a margin note (contribution).
  \item \textbf{Or open a bounty} --- e.g. “Suggest a sharper tagline” (40 KO Coins) or “Find a real community example” (60 KO Coins).
  \item \textbf{Submit} --- author reviews; if accepted/valuable $\rightarrow$ credit on book + KO Coins in wallet.
  \item \textbf{Profile} --- see contributions, badges, KO Coin balance.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Journey B --- The community participant (Phase 2 features)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item From a book hub $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Community} tab.
  \item \textbf{Vote in a poll} --- cover choice, plot direction, pacing, ending.
  \item \textbf{Post a fan theory} --- title + body; others upvote.
  \item \textbf{Join a story circle} --- beta readers, lore, editing, or general discussion.
  \item \textbf{Read “Behind the story”} --- author posts on process, deleted scenes, research.
  \item \textbf{Check Timeline} --- see how the book evolved (chapter drops, milestones, rewards).
  \item \textbf{Check Credits} --- see who shaped the book (inline + bounty contributors).
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Journey C --- The author (mention lightly in “for authors” content)}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Authors are \textbf{invited by Kommuniti admin} (not self-serve at launch).
  \item They manage books via \textbf{Author Desk}: chapters, bounties, review contributions, run polls, close challenges.
  \item Message for author-recruitment posters: \textit{“Interested in publishing on KO Reads? Contact the Kommuniti team.”}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{16. KO Coins --- explain simply for creators}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{What:} Kommuniti’s community currency (shown as (KO Coin) in navbar and profile).

  \item \textbf{How to talk about it:} “Recognition you can measure” --- not “get rich”.

\end{itemize}
